Meet Coral Santiago

Raised on Puerto Rico's vibrant north coast, Coral Santiago established Pronoia Realty to offer an alternative to high-volume corporate brokerages. With a Master's degree, entrepreneurial background, and deep roots across the island, she brings a warm, highly personal, and strategic approach to buyer representation.

Fluent in English and Spanish, Coral specializes in helping mainlanders, investors, and Act 60 decree holders seamlessly navigate cultural transitions, legal systems, and property customization.

Geographic Expertise

Coral provides intimate, boots-on-the-ground knowledge across Puerto Rico's most coveted coastal corridors:
